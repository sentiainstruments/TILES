SENTIA Instruments

Quick start guide

24 expressive pads, six buttons, one grid for playing, sequencing and launching clips. Here's everything you need to start, on a few pages.

02

Plug in

TILES runs from USB alone. Add the 12 V supply for full brightness and the CV outputs.

USB-C

Power and MIDI over one USB-C cable. Shows up as two MIDI ports: SENTIA TILES MIDI (play and record from this one) and SENTIA TILES DAW (for the Ableton script only).

12 V power

Barrel jack, centre positive. Use a 12 V, 3 A supply. Brightest lights and the CV/gate outputs; USB MIDI keeps working when both are connected.

MIDI in / out

1/8" (3.5 mm) TRS for hardware synths and drum machines. Out sends what USB sends; in follows clock. Type A by default, Type B selectable in settings.

Pedal

1/4" (6.35 mm) jack. Sustain by default, or an expression pedal. Works with normally-open and normally-closed pedals (settings).

CV · gate

1/8" (3.5 mm) jacks: pitch CV (1 V/octave), pressure CV (0–10 V) and gate. Only with 12 V power connected.

03

Choose a mode

Opens the mode menu. Click again to close it.
Press a pad
Switches to that mode. The current mode pulses white.

Melodic magenta · Sequencer red · Bass guitar amber · Chord blue · Song yellow (coming soon) · Ableton teal.

A running sequence keeps playing when you switch modes.

04

Melodic mode

The default. Notes rise left to right, and each row continues from the one below. The lowest note is bottom-left.

Octave down / up, ±3. The button's blink pattern shows how far.
+
Transpose. Press both together: the key letter shows on the pads. Minus / plus change the key; both again to exit.
+
Scale selector (next page).
Pitch bend on / off. Tilt a pad to bend.

Pedal tricks

Pedal
Sustain, sent the standard way (works with MPE synths).
Harmonics
Hold the pedal and one note, then lightly touch other pads: each plucks an overtone of the held note.
05

Scales

Every playing mode follows the selected scale. The layout keeps its shape: in a scale, the pads only play notes of that scale.

+
Open the scale selector (hold circle, click triangle).
Press a pad
Selects that scale.
Row 1
Chromatic · Major · Minor · Dorian · Lydian · Mixolydian
Row 2
Blues major · Blues minor · Arabian · Diminished · Pentatonic major · Pentatonic minor
Row 3
Egyptian · Whole tone · Japanese (Miyako-bushi)
16–24
Custom scale slots, coming with the companion app.
06

Chord mode

Chords on the left two columns, a melody grid on the right four.

Columns 1–2
8 chords from the current scale, bottom-left up: I, ii, iii, IV, V, vi, vii°, then I an octave higher.
Strike
A full seventh chord with a bass note. Strike speed sets the loudness.
Columns 3–6
Melody, 4 × 4, starting on the root at the bottom-left of the block.
+
Change the scale. Scales without 7 notes use major chords.

Chords are one octave below the melody and go out on MIDI channel 12, so they can play a different synth.

07

Bass guitar mode

A four-string bass neck: rows are strings, columns are frets.

Rows
Strings, standard bass tuning: G, D, A, E from top to bottom.
Columns
Frets. Column 1 starts as the open string.
Slide along the neck one fret at a time.
Markers
Glowing pads show the fret dots, like inlays.
08

Sequencer

24 pads, 24 steps, read like a page: left to right, top to bottom. Four lanes play at once.

Tap a step
On / off.
Hold a step
Pick its note: tap any pad. Keep holding and press to set the step's probability.
+step
Ratchet: repeats inside the step.
/
Start / stop.
+
Pattern length, 1–24 steps.
×4
Tap tempo when no DAW clock is coming in. Circle blinks on the beat.
+
Capture: play, and your notes are written into the steps on the beat. Same again to finish.

Pattern bank

Open / close the bank.
Press a slot
That lane plays that pattern, and it's the one you edit.
+tap
Save the slot (green flash).
+hold 3 s
Clear the slot (red flash).

Lanes 1–4 play on MIDI channels 16, 15, 14, 13. Patterns are saved on the device and follow a DAW's clock when one is running.

09

Ableton mode

Launch clips and scenes in Ableton Live, with Live's own clip colours on the pads.

Touch
Feel it: a haptic click where there's a clip.
Press
Launch the clip. Press a playing clip to stop it.
Press empty
Arms the track and records a new clip. TILES switches to melodic mode so you can play it.
+
Stop all clips. While recording a new clip: finish it.
+hold 3 s
Delete that clip.
Move the five-track window left / right.

Dim = has a clip · pulsing = playing · fast blink = queued.

Transport (every mode except sequencer)

Click
Play / stop Live.
Hold 2 s
Release to start recording.

TILES DISPLAY

A Max for Live device. Put it before a track's instrument and click VIEW: the notes that track plays light up on TILES in melodic mode. Two tracks can show at once (the second in soft red).

One-time setup in Live

1
Copy the TILES script folder into Live's User Library → Remote Scripts, then restart Live.
2
Settings → Link, Tempo & MIDI → Control Surface: TILES, input and output SENTIA TILES (DAW).
3
Input SENTIA TILES (MIDI): Track and MPE on. Output: Sync on for tempo.

Expression & feel

The square "sentia" button tunes how TILES responds.

Pitch bend on / off (square glows when on).
Hold
Expression menu. Each row is a slider: press a column, 1 = least, 6 = most, 4 = default. Haptics column 1 = off.

Haptic strength, without opening anything else.
+ 2 s
MPE on / off. Off = plain MIDI on channel 1 for any synth; the square breathes to remind you.

13

Sleep & wake

Idle 1 min
Screensaver animations (20 min in the sequencer).
Idle 20 min
Deep sleep: everything dark, circle pulses slowly.
Hold 4 s
Screensaver now. browse animations.
Hold 8 s
Deep sleep now, and a MIDI panic: all notes off, sustain released.
Wake
Touch a pad or press any button.

Stuck note? Hold circle for 8 seconds. It silences every channel, then wake TILES and keep playing.

On USB power the lights run dimmer and CV/gate stay off. On 12 V you get full brightness and the CV outputs.
